## Our Display Technologies

### 1. ePaper Displays

Monochrome, multi-color, and full-color ePaper display modules for low-power embedded systems, electronic shelf labels, digital signage, smart devices, and industrial applications.

Engineering resources cover SPI interfaces, display drivers, refresh control, and MCU integration.

**[Explore ePaper Displays](https://www.nosedisplay.com/product/epaper-displays/)**

### 2. Rotary & Round Displays

Round TFT LCD, AMOLED, and rotary knob display modules for smart home controls, industrial instruments, interactive interfaces, and embedded products.

Options include capacitive touch, rotary encoders, controller integration, and customized display interfaces.

**[Explore Rotary & Round Displays](https://www.nosedisplay.com/product/round-lcds/)**

### 3. ESP32 Displays

ESP32-S3 and ESP32-P4 display solutions for IoT devices, embedded GUI development, smart control panels, and connected display applications.

Engineering topics include touch control, display initialization, Wi-Fi connectivity, and LVGL integration.

**[Explore ESP32 Displays](https://www.nosedisplay.com/product/esp32-displays/)**

### 4. Industrial HMI Displays

UART HMI touchscreens, programmable HMI panels, and industrial Panel PCs for factory automation, machine control, EV charging equipment, and energy systems.

Supported integration options vary by model and include RS-232, RS-485, CAN, Ethernet, touch interfaces, and customized GUI solutions.

**[Explore Industrial HMI Displays](https://www.nosedisplay.com/product/hmi-displays/)**

### 5. Industrial Displays

Industrial TFT LCD modules and large-format display panels for factory automation, medical equipment, kiosks, industrial terminals, and embedded control systems.

Solutions include high-brightness displays, wide-temperature options, LVDS/eDP interfaces, touch integration, and OEM mechanical customization.

**[Explore Industrial Displays](https://www.nosedisplay.com/product/industrial-displays/)**
